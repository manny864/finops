# Subprocessors

CSCloudSolutions uses the following third-party processors to deliver its services

> Note: All subprocessors have agreed to data protection terms equivalent to the GDPR. We notify customers of new subprocessors and provide 30 days to object.

## Data Residency
CSCloudSolutions operates a single physical deployment, hosted in the United States (Azure West US 2). We do not yet offer regional data residency selection — all customer data is processed and stored in this single location regardless of the customer's own region.

- 🇺🇸 United States: Azure West US 2

Planned: Multi-region data residency (EU/US/LATAM/APAC) is a potential future enhancement, not yet scheduled. This page will be updated with a firm timeline once multi-region deployment is planned.

## Processor Details

| Processor | Purpose | Locations |
|---|---|---|
| Microsoft Azure | Cloud compute, storage, networking, and data processing | United States |
| MySQL Database Host | Relational database hosting and backups | United States |
| Paddle | Payment processing, billing, and subscription management | United States / United Kingdom |
| WorkOS | SAML 2.0 SSO/authentication and identity management | United States |
| Google Gemini AI | LLM inference for recommendations (optional, Bring-Your-Own-Key supported) | United States |
| Microsoft Graph | Email and calendar integration for notifications | United States |
| Cloudflare | CDN, edge WAF, and DDoS protection | Global edge network, origin in United States |

Data Protection: This processor has committed to processing data in compliance with GDPR and equivalent standards.
DPA Available: Yes. Standard Contractual Clauses included in their data processing agreements.

## Subprocessor Change Policy
We may change or add subprocessors at any time. You will be notified in advance via email (at least 30 days prior), and you have the right to:
- Review the new processor's security and privacy practices
- Object to the addition within 30 days
- Terminate your subscription if we add a processor you cannot accept
